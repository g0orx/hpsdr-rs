openHPSDR client · v0.15.2

hpsdr-rs

A free, open-source SDR client for openHPSDR radios and more, written in Rust with egui and using WDSP for the signal processing. It runs on Linux (including the Raspberry Pi), Windows and macOS. GPL-3.0-or-later, by John Melton G0ORX.

Supported radios

  • openHPSDR Protocol 1 and 2 over Ethernet: Metis, Hermes, Angelia, Orion and Orion2 (the ANAN range, 10E to 8000DLE) and Hermes Lite 2.
  • Original Ozy/Mercury/Penny over USB, with the firmware loaded from bundled files.
  • ADALM-Pluto and clones, over the network. TX not yet on air
  • Receive only: RX-888 Mk2, RTL-SDR dongles (direct sampling below 24 MHz) and MSI.SDR / MSi2500 sticks. Several dongles at once, each kept by USB port.
  • Discovery by broadcast on every network interface, including APIPA links, or by IP address.
  • Settings saved per radio, keyed by its MAC address.

Receive

  • Main receiver plus extra receiver windows, each with its own VFO, mode, filter and AF gain.
  • Spectrum and waterfall with Click-to-Tune, adjustable dB range, palettes and automatic floor tracking.
  • Tune by scrolling the digit under the mouse, by the VFO step on the spectrum, or by tapping a VFO to type a frequency.
  • SSB, CW, AM, FM and digital modes. Filter, AF gain and mode remembered per mode and per band; band stack with 3 memories per band.
  • NB/NB2, NR/NR2/NNR, SNB and squelch. AGC Off, Long, Slow, Medium or Fast, with adjustable gain.
  • Diversity reception on radios with two ADCs, and an RX equalizer.
  • Built-in CW decoder, and recording to WAV.

Transmit

  • Mic audio through WDSP's TX chain with ALC, compressor, TX equalizer and power/SWR/ALC meters. Also works with no local mic (CW, TCI audio, the radio's own mic).
  • PureSignal PA linearisation on both protocols, switchable live.
  • 9-point PA calibration per band, Tune at a separate reduced power, two-tone test, TX spectrum.
  • Dual VFO with Split and Duplex, TX tail delay.
  • FM repeater shift, CTCSS, and FM deviation of 5 or 2.5 kHz.
  • CW: radio keyer settings with break-in, PC sidetone with smooth edges, five stored messages, TX offset by the CW pitch.

Station integration

  • TCI (modelled on Thetis), rigctl (Hamlib) and Kenwood TS-2000 CAT for WSJT-X, N1MM+, Log4OM and similar. CAT also on up to 3 serial ports for amplifiers.
  • MIDI control surfaces with learn mode and Thetis Midi2Cat import.
  • Raspberry Pi front panels: piHPSDR's Controller 1, 2 V1, 2 V2 and G2.
  • Open-collector outputs, filter boards (Alex, Apollo, Charly25, N2ADR), per-band RX/TX antennas, HL2 IO board and audio codec add-on.
  • Selectable audio devices, for virtual audio cables.

VHF, UHF and satellite

  • Transverters up to 10 GHz, for QO-100 through an LNB.
  • QO-100 beacon monitor that locks the LNB's frequency.
  • DVB-S2 DATV receiver, with the video sent to VLC.
  • 2m, 70cm and 23cm bands for the dongles and the Pluto.

Remote operation

  • Web interface built in: operate from a phone, tablet or another computer's browser, with nothing to install. Spectrum, waterfall, tuning, receive audio, and transmit with the phone's mic.
  • CW from the browser: send text and saved messages, or key live with on-screen paddles or a real key through a USB keyboard adapter.
  • HTTPS and a password, a compact layout for phones, and about 150 kbit/s of compressed audio.
  • hpsdr-remote, the same client as a native program, including on a Raspberry Pi with a front-panel controller.
  • Connects to hpsdr-rs over TCI, with extensions for the spectrum and compressed audio.

Also

  • Compact layout for 7" displays, chosen automatically by screen size, and full screen.
  • FPGA firmware updates (bootloader or in-application) and static IP setup.
  • Installers: Windows .msi; macOS for Apple Silicon; Linux AppImage, .deb and .rpm for x86_64 and ARM64.
  • A 25-chapter user manual, also as a PDF.

Always bench-test into a dummy load at reduced drive before transmitting into an antenna, especially after a new build.
